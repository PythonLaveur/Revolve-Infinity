# Burrower

Burrower uses the Miner reference: 773 HP, 80 armor, 30 magic resistance, 32.6 attack range, 143 sight range, fast movement (61.2 per turn), and 600 Elixir. His defenses favor armor while preserving roughly 115% of the Miner reference's effective physical HP. His physical basic attack has 30 fixed armor penetration. All his damage against arena structures (turrets and bases) is reduced by 50%.

## Abilities

- **Tunnel Pounce** — select an enemy troop within 140 units. Burrower sets it as its focus and dashes along a passable route into basic attack range. Costs 100 AP.
- **Dust Veil** — aim a 45-degree cone up to 61.2 units away. Enemy troops in the cone take magical damage scaling with Burrower's ATK and are silenced for 4 global turn ends. Costs 200 AP.
- **Deep Tunnel** — choose any point on the map. Burrower disappears for 2 global turn ends, then emerges at that point or the nearest free ground position. Its next basic attack against a troop launches the target into the air if used within 4 global turn ends after emergence. Costs 300 AP.

The dash range and airborne duration remain prototype values where the design did not specify numbers.
