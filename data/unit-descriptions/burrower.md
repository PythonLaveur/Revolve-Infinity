# Burrower

Burrower is a 600-Elixir fast melee hero with 773 HP, 80 armor, 30 magic resistance, 32.6 attack range and 143 sight range. His physical basic attacks scale with ATK and have 30 flat armor penetration. He deals half damage to arena structures.

## Abilities

- **Tunnel Pounce** — select an enemy troop within 140 units to make it Burrower's focus, then dash into basic attack range. Costs 100 AP.
- **Dust Veil** — aim a 45-degree cone up to 61.2 units away. Enemies in it take magic damage scaling with Burrower's HP and are silenced for four global turn ends. Costs 150 AP.
- **Deep Tunnel** — choose a point on the map. Burrower disappears for two global turn ends, then emerges there or at the nearest available position. His next basic attack against a troop launches it into the air if used within four turns. Costs 200 AP.
