# Alchemist

Alchemist is a 600-Elixir ranged placeholder hero with 590 HP, 45 Force, 54 ATK, 35 armor, 60 magic resistance, 110 attack range, and 34 movement per turn. Its basic attacks deal physical damage and can target ground and air.

## Abilities

- **Guard** — gain a shield equal to 14% of maximum HP for three turns. Costs 150 AP.
- **Surge** — increase AC generation by 35% for three turns. Costs 200 AP.
- **Strike** — deal physical area damage scaling with ATK to enemies within a 30-unit radius, at a cast range of 122.4 units. Costs 250 AP.

This is a placeholder kit. See [Alchemist's stat module](../stats/alchemist.js) for current balance data.
