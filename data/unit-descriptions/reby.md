# Reby

Reby is an immobile 800-Elixir building with 678 HP, 50 armor, 40 magic resistance and 15.1 HP decay at each global turn end. She has no basic attack or sight range. Every 13 turns, she launches a wave of three autonomous flying Kamikaze Drones toward the nearest enemy target.

Each drone has 4.5 tiles of sight and 2.5 tiles of attack range. When an enemy enters attack range, the drone becomes untargetable and rushes that target during the same turn. It explodes on contact, dealing magic damage equal to 350% of Reby's ATK in a 40.8-unit radius.

## Abilities

- **Drone Foundry** — place a fragile building within 4 arena tiles of Reby. It spawns one weak Spear Goblin equivalent every 4 global turns. Costs 400 AP.
- **Guard Foundry** — place a fragile building within 4 arena tiles of Reby. It spawns one weak melee guard every 13 global turns. Costs 500 AP.
- **Aegis Dome** — create a 160-unit radius dome around Reby for 14 global turn ends. It absorbs ranged damage originating outside until its shield is depleted. Costs 400 AP.
