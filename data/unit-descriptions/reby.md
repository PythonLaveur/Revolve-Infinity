# Reby

Reby uses the pre-rework Furnace reference: an immobile 800-Elixir building with 678 HP (20% above its resistance-converted reference), 50 armor/MR, and 15.1 HP decay per global turn end. It requires no focus: for 70 AC it sends three autonomous flying kamikaze drones toward the nearest enemy. Reby starts with 50 AC and gains 15 AC on each of its turns; remaining AC carries over. The three drones spawn in a spaced line in front of Reby. At the start of each of their turns, they move 81.6 units (the Very Fast / Fire Spirit movement class) and then stop until their next turn. Their movement is animated like other troops. On contact, they explode for magic splash damage (40.8-unit radius) equal to 160% of Reby's ATK per enemy hit. At current stats, each drone deals 44 damage to a Ninja with 50 magic resistance; three hits deal 132 damage and can kill one Ninja without a shield.

## Abilities

- **Drone Foundry** — place a building within 4 arena tiles (108.8 units) of Reby; its HP and decay scale from Reby's HP. It creates a ranged Scout every 2 global turn ends. Scouts spawn in front of the building and have 122.4 attack range (60% of a standard turret's range); their HP and ATK scale from Reby's stats. Costs 400 AP.
- **Guard Foundry** — place a building within 4 arena tiles (108.8 units) of Reby; its HP and decay scale from Reby's HP. It creates a tougher melee Guard every 2 global turn ends. Guards spawn in front of the building; their HP and ATK scale from Reby's stats. Costs 500 AP.
- **Aegis Dome** — self-target; surrounds Reby with a 160-unit radius dome for 14 global turn ends. It absorbs up to 120% of Reby's HP in ranged damage originating outside the dome. Costs 400 AP.

The drone speed and dome radius remain prototype values where the concept did not specify numbers.
