# Rayka

Rayka uses the Baby Dragon reference: 1060 HP, 25 armor/MR, 95.2 attack range (3.5 Clash Royale tiles), 143 sight range, fast movement (61.2 per turn), and 800 Elixir. She is built around a larger HP pool with lower resistances. She can attack ground and air. Each energy grants 1.5% multiplicative bonus armor and magic resistance.

## Flight

Rayka can be deployed over ground troops and terrain, and flies over terrain, structures, and ground troops while moving. Other air troops block her path and deployment. Ground attackers whose basic attack cannot target air do not acquire her as a target. Defensive structures still target flying troops.

## Abilities

- **Basic attack — Thunderstorm:** summons a radius-40.8 storm at the target. It deals magic damage equal to 31% of Rayka's APT before mitigation at each global turn end for 2 turns. Storms hit enemy troops and buildings. Every storm is independent, overlapping storms stack their damage, and the storm boundary, lightning marks, and remaining duration are clearly shown in the arena.
- **Relay Flight** — only while Rayka has no focus; select an ally within 600 units. Rayka teleports to a free position at or near the ally and both gain 50% movement speed for 4 real-time seconds. The speed bonus increases movement budget and movement animation speed.
- **Aegis Feedback** — self-targeted; for 10 global turn ends, Rayka heals for 30% of health damage dealt by her storms. Each storm hit on an enemy unit grants 5 energy; buildings do not generate energy.
- **Lightning Storm** — self-targeted; consumes all energy and empowers Rayka for 10 global turn ends. Her storms last 4 turns and gain 1% radius and damage per energy consumed. Enemies inside a storm cannot receive manual movement commands while inside it. When a storm damages a building, it creates a secondary storm centered there; secondary storms do not chain further.

Energy remains after Aegis ends. After two global turn ends without energy generation, Rayka loses 10% of her stored energy at each turn end until she generates energy again.
