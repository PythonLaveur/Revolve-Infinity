# Rayka

Rayka is an 800-Elixir flying mage with 1060 HP, 35 armor, 25 magic resistance, 95.2 attack range, 143 sight range and 61.2 movement per turn. Her basic attack creates a lingering thunderstorm at the target. Storms damage troops and buildings; damage to arena structures is halved. Energy increases her defenses.

## Abilities

- **Relay Flight** — only while Rayka has no focus, teleport to an ally anywhere on the map. Both units gain 50% movement speed for four seconds. Costs 200 AP.
- **Aegis Feedback** — for 12 global turn ends, Rayka heals for 30% of damage dealt by her storms. Each storm hit on a unit grants 7 energy. Costs 250 AP.
- **Lightning Storm** — consume all energy. For 10 global turn ends, empower Rayka's storms: they last four turns and gain radius and damage based on the energy consumed. Only while this skill is active, enemies damaged inside a storm are confused. Storms that hit buildings create one secondary storm there. Costs 400 AP.
