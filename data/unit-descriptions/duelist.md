# Duelist

Duelist is a 700-Elixir melee placeholder hero with 700 HP, 85 Force, 130 ATK, 45 armor and magic resistance, and 48 movement per turn. Its basic attacks deal physical damage.

## Abilities

- **Guard** — gain a shield equal to 14% of maximum HP for three turns. Costs 150 AP.
- **Surge** — increase AC generation by 35% for three turns. Costs 200 AP.
- **Strike** — deal physical area damage scaling with ATK to enemies within a 30-unit radius, at a cast range of 122.4 units. Costs 250 AP.

This is a placeholder kit. See [Duelist's stat module](../stats/duelist.js) for current balance data.
