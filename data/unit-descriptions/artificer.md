# Artificer

Artificer is a 600-Elixir defensive placeholder hero with 740 HP, 72 Force, 64 ATK, 60 armor, 55 magic resistance, 90 attack range, and 28 movement per turn. Its basic attacks deal physical damage and can target ground and air.

## Abilities

- **Guard** — gain a shield equal to 14% of maximum HP for three turns. Costs 150 AP.
- **Surge** — increase AC generation by 35% for three turns. Costs 200 AP.
- **Strike** — deal physical area damage scaling with ATK to enemies within a 30-unit radius, at a cast range of 122.4 units. Costs 250 AP.

This is a placeholder kit. See [Artificer's stat module](../stats/artificer.js) for current balance data.
