# Ninja

Ninja is one 600-Elixir card that deploys seven independent melee fighters. Each has 124 HP, 50 armor/MR, 20 attack range, fast movement (61.2 per turn), and magic attacks. Each member is balanced as roughly two level-11 Skeletons in health and damage; the seven-member squad therefore remains close to the original 15-Skeleton Army total. Members can each be targeted or defeated individually.

## Abilities

- **Piercing Shuriken** — self-target; grants the squad 60% magic penetration for 4 global turn ends. Costs 100 AP.
- **Paralyzing Strikes** — self-target; the squad's attacks add one mark to enemy troops for 10 global turn ends. At 10 marks, the target is stunned for 2 global turn ends. Costs 200 AP.
- **Shadow Replicas** — self-target; each original Ninja creates a clone after a basic attack, up to 2 living clones per original. Clones attack automatically and cannot be controlled or create more clones. Prototype cost: 300 AP. The duration was unspecified, so the effect currently lasts for the rest of the match.
