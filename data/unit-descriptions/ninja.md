# Ninja

Ninja is one 500-Elixir card that deploys seven independent melee fighters. Each has 134 HP, 20 armor, 15 magic resistance, 20 attack range, 143 sight range and fast movement. Their basic attacks deal magic damage. Members can be targeted or defeated individually.

## Abilities

- **Piercing Shuriken** — grants the squad 60% magic penetration for four global turn ends. Costs 100 AP.
- **Paralyzing Strikes** — for 10 global turn ends, squad attacks mark enemy troops. At 10 marks, the target is stunned for two global turn ends. Costs 200 AP.
- **Shadow Replicas** — each original Ninja creates a clone after a basic attack, up to two living clones per original. Clones attack automatically, cannot be controlled and cannot create further clones. Costs 300 AP.
