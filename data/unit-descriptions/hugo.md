# Hugo

Hugo uses the Giant reference: 3968 HP, 168 Force/ATK, 30 armor/MR, 40 attack range, 204 sight range, slow movement (30.6 per turn), and 1000 Elixir. His effective HP is 130% of the level-11 Giant reference. He prioritizes buildings in sight and ignores enemy troops while a building is visible; otherwise, he can acquire and fight troops. Each basic attack grants 10 energy. He also gains 1 energy for every 1% of his maximum HP he actually loses.

## Passive: Change of Turf

Each time Hugo reaches 50 energy, he consumes 50 and creates or refreshes a grass patch centered on himself. Its radius is 68 units (2.5 arena tiles) and it lasts 10 global turn ends; excess energy carries over. The river and living buildings remain unaffected. Allied troops standing on valid grass receive 40% stronger buffs and healing.

## Abilities

- **Dab** — self-target; requires a building within Hugo's basic attack range. Restores HP based on 12% of Hugo's maximum HP and grants 10 energy. Select the card, then click anywhere in the arena. Costs 150 AP.
- **Smoke Grenade** — area-target; launches a projectile that creates a radius-100 smoke area. Enemy troops hit have their attack range halved for 8 turn ends. Their focus is cleared if its target is now out of their reduced attack range. Costs 200 AP.
- **Bodyguard** — ally-target; select another friendly troop within 120 units. For 8 global turn ends, Hugo moves 30% slower and raises a 163.2-unit-wide wall perpendicular to the line toward the selected ally. Ranged attacks crossing the wall are redirected to Hugo after flat damage reduction equal to 40% of his Force (20% for arena turrets and bases). Direct damage to Hugo receives the same reduction. If the reduced ranged hit is below 2% of his maximum HP, Hugo gains 5 energy and grants 5 AC to the selected ally, or the nearest living ally if that ally has died. Summoned minion hits grant only 1 AC. Costs 400 AP.

Prototype values not specified in the original concept are the ally selection range, smoke radius/range reduction, and smoke projectile speed.
