# Hugo

Hugo is a slow 1000-Elixir building hunter with 3968 HP, 168 Force and ATK, 30 armor and magic resistance, 40 attack range, and 204 sight range. He prioritizes buildings while one is visible. Attacks and lost health generate energy; at each 50 energy he creates or refreshes grass around himself, which strengthens allied healing and buffs.

## Abilities

- **Dab** — when a building is within Hugo's attack range, restore health equal to 12% of his maximum HP and gain 10 energy. Costs 150 AP.
- **Smoke Grenade** — target a point within 7 arena tiles. The projectile deals light physical damage scaling with ATK and leaves a visible field for eight global turn ends. Enemy troops inside have half attack range and half sight range. Costs 200 AP.
- **Bodyguard** — select an ally within 120 units. For eight global turn ends, Hugo moves 30% slower and raises a wall in front of himself, perpendicular to his facing. The wall redirects ranged hits to Hugo and reduces their damage. Costs 400 AP.
